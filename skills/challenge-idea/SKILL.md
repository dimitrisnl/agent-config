# Challenge an idea

- Identify the goal, constraints, and assumptions behind the proposal.
- Challenge consequential assumptions using counterexamples, failure scenarios, and available evidence.
- Ask a few high-impact questions at a time. Look up accessible facts rather than asking the user to supply them.
- Explain your strongest objections and recommend alternatives. Do not manufacture disagreement.
- Stop when the major risks and unresolved decisions are clear. Summarize what holds up, what needs changing, and what remains unknown.
